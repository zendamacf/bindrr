Redesigned the card details modal. Price, finish and quantity now sit at the top beside the card image, with a 30-day price sparkline and a Save button right under the fields. Setting the quantity to 0 turns Save into "Remove from collection". Card text is easier to scan, and Remove moved into a "more actions" menu away from Save. You can step to the previous or next card with the arrow buttons or the left and right arrow keys. On mobile, Save stays pinned at the bottom, and you can click to zoom on the card image.
